# Verified Datasets and Benchmarks

- **[SciFact](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2020.emnlp-main.609)** — scientific claims paired with abstract-level evidence and support/refute labels.
- **[PubMedQA](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/D19-1259)** — biomedical research questions derived from PubMed abstracts with yes/no/maybe answers.
- **[TruthfulQA](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2022.acl-long.229)** — adversarial questions designed to test whether models reproduce common false beliefs.
- **[HaluEval](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.397)** — a large-scale hallucination evaluation collection.
- **[GPQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12022)** — difficult graduate-level questions in biology, physics and chemistry.
- **[Med-HALT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15343)** — medical-domain hallucination and reasoning evaluation.
- **[FreshQA/FreshLLMs](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.findings-acl.813)** — time-sensitive and false-premise questions for testing factual freshness.
